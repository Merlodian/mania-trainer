xdc7.dll rates 7K osu!mania charts. It is built from xdc7.cpp, which is XDC1
(TheBagelOfMan's 4K osu!mania difficulty algorithm) widened to the three and
four fingers a hand uses in 7K. It keeps XDC1's method and settings, and it
rates rice only: a long note counts as a tap.

The 7K changes were made for mania trainer. They are not TheBagelOfMan's work
and he has not reviewed them, so a 7K rating is not an XDC1 rating.
